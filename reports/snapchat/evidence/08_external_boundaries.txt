Download trigger:
  com.snap.bloops.data.DownloadBloopsAiModelsDurableJob
    @DurableJobIdentifier(identifier="DOWNLOAD_BLOOPS_AI_MODELS", metadataType=...)
  com.snap.bloops.net.BloopsHttpInterface (network layer)
  libclient.so URLs / hosts: https://lens-storage.storage.googleapis.com,
    https://perception.sc-cdn.net, https://bolt-gcdn.sc-cdn.net, *.sc-cdn.net

What crosses the boundary (inferred from these contracts):
  - Snap servers -> device: encrypted model bytes (*.dnn.enc) and bloops/TNN
    native component; model URL/version metadata.
  - Device -> server: model download requests (model id/version); no user image
    is passed to ModelCrypto/FaceNeutrality APIs.
  - Decryption happens on device with a key/IV supplied by the bloops native
    library; plaintext model bytes are fed to the on-device interpreter.
  - Inference itself is local (MACE/TNN/NNAPI/TFLite backend in bloops).
