      u[i] = byte at UV+2i      (second byte)
      v[i] = byte at UV+2i-1    (first byte)
  => interleaved plane is V,U (NV21); separate U/V arrays feed the GLES renderer.

ASVL_OFFSCREEN layout used by all three paths:
  +0x00 u32 pixel_format = 1 (NV21)
  +0x04 s32 width
  +0x08 s32 height
  +0x10 u8* plane0 (Y)
  +0x18 u8* plane1 (VU)
  +0x30/+0x34 s32 pitch[0]/pitch[1]
